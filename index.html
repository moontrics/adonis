What changed under the hood

The brace cue

A full-body brace — ribs down, posterior pelvic tilt, glutes engaged, breathing through it — became the running standard on every lift. Not a cue layered on top. A level change.

  • Diagnosed mid-session on scap pulls: cutting the cue stack from seven things to two (glutes + crossed ankles) killed the swing instantly.
  • Transferred straight into clean assisted pull-ups (x8/8/8, full ownership) and unlocked the negatives — from zero top control to holding the top on 9 of 10 reps.
  • Physical confirmations: face-pull rope slip stopped when the brace held, captain's chair joints went quiet.
  • Pre-set ritual now standard: build the brace on the ground first, then grip the bar.
Named honestly

The bottleneck

The training side is no longer the limiter. The side profile is. Two levers, neither about lifting more.

01Pelvic tilt

Reduced steadily since Week 0 but still present in the Week 12 side view. The unlock is the brace cue plus the daily corrective protocol — hip flexor stretches, glute bridges, dead bugs — run at the top of every gym day.

02Lower belly

Training-day protein lands well (170g+ on the last PB day). The gap is rest-day and weekend consistency. After twelve weeks of upper-body recomp, this is now the lever that moves the side profile.

The win worth marking

Override → discipline

The early-weeks override pattern — pushing past agreed caps when momentum felt good, technique slipping — has matured into stretch-with-discipline. This week the 20kg single arm row went past the stated cap, but the brace held and the reps were clean: a judgement call with full information, not an ego override. And the 45kg lat pulldown stopped at x8 because rep quality dropped, not because the body failed. Stopping early to protect form is the inverse of the old pattern.

Weeks 13–16

Next block

  • Open pull days with the PBs. 42.5kg seated row and 20kg face pulls go first — this week proved 20kg anything doesn't hold clean late in a session.
  • Drop pull-up assistance to 30kg now that x8/8/8 is owned at 35kg.
  • Add unilateral left-led pressing. The left arm is the governor on bilateral PBs; close the gap so it stops capping the lifts.
  • Tricep pushdowns earlier in the push order so the x10+ at 15kg ownership can be hit fresh.
  • The belly is now the headline lever. Twelve weeks of upper-body recomp have done their job. The next visible jump comes from nutrition consistency on rest days and weekends.
